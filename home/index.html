What this does not claim

That containment is proven. That the model is safe, honest, or wise. That a checker replaces human judgment. The gate is measured and its failure rates are published: in a preregistered battery it caught 3 of 24 injected faults before repair and 20 of 24 after, while flagging 4 of 16 clean statements. Those numbers are on the dashboard, next to the runs they came from.
