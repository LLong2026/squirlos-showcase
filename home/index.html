It covers the writes that pass through it, not every write on every system, and we say so on the page rather than after the sale.

What this does not claim

That containment is proven. That the model is safe, honest, or wise. That a checker replaces human judgment. The gate is measured and its failure rates are published, including the unflattering ones. In a preregistered Monte Carlo run on 2026-11-17 it caught 3 of 24 injected faults before repair and 20 of 24 after, while flagging 4 of 16 clean statements. That run is recorded in the doctrine of record rather than on the dashboard; the dashboard publishes the battery figures it does carry: BLIND-1 4 of 6 repelled, BLIND-2 16 of 20 repelled, and zero escapes across four escape batteries.
